**Excel Sales Performance Dashboard
Project Overview**

This project presents an interactive sales performance dashboard developed in Microsoft Excel. It analyses 112,036 sales records from 2011 to 2016 across six countries and three product categories.

The dashboard enables users to monitor overall performance and explore revenue, profit, cost, units sold, monthly trends, country performance and top-performing products.

**Key Performance Indicators**
Total Revenue: ₹84.8M
Total Profit: ₹32.0M
Total Cost: ₹52.8M
Units Sold: 1.33M
Overall Profit Margin: 37.8%


**Dashboard Features**
Interactive month slicer
Monthly revenue and profit analysis
Revenue comparison by country
Product revenue and profitability analysis
Top 10 products by revenue
Dynamic KPI cards connected to PivotTables

**Business Insights**

The business generated ₹84.8M in revenue and ₹32.0M in profit, resulting in a 37.8% overall profit margin.
The United States generated the highest country revenue at approximately ₹27.8M, representing 32.7% of total revenue.
Australia generated approximately ₹21.2M. Together, the United States and Australia contributed 57.7% of total revenue.
Bikes generated approximately ₹61.4M, accounting for 72.4% of total revenue.
December was the strongest month, generating approximately ₹9.05M in revenue.
July was the lowest-performing month, generating approximately ₹5.69M.
Road-150 Red, 62 was the highest-revenue product, generating approximately ₹3.81M.
The top 10 products contributed approximately 35.6% of total revenue.
